About the Project

pQT Protocol is the first cryptocurrency that brings Central Bank Quantitative Tightening (QT) logic into fully immutable tokenomics on Solana, built on the Token-2022 standard. The protocol uses the built-in Transfer Fee extension and hands the right to withdraw withheld fees to a Program Derived Address (PDA) controlled entirely by the smart contract — full technical details are in the project's README.

💸

1. Fee Accrual (0.05%)

On every $pQT transfer, the Token-2022 program automatically withholds 5 basis points (0.05%) of the transferred amount on the recipient's token account, as a withheld balance (withheld_amount).

🔐

2. Decentralized Collection

The withdraw_withheld_authority is assigned to the program's PDA (seeds = [b"vault-authority"]). The PDA has no private key — the program signs on its behalf, so fees can never be withdrawn outside the contract.

⚙️

3. harvest_and_distribute

Any user or bot can call this public instruction. In a single atomic transaction it harvests withheld fees, burns 80%, pays an instant 15% bounty to the caller, and sends the remaining 5% to the Dev Treasury.

Permissionless by Design

The team holds no separate key for collecting or withdrawing accumulated fees — that operation is only possible through the program itself, and anyone can trigger it. Freeze Authority is permanently revoked. Mint Authority and Fee Config Authority are currently held by the team and will be revoked once tokenomics parameters are finalized; verify the current status anytime with spl-token display or on Solscan.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about $pQT mechanics and security.

What makes $pQT tokenomics genuinely deflationary?
Unlike standard tokens, $pQT utilizes Solana Token-2022 Transfer Fee extensions. Every transfer holds back 0.05%, and 80% of those withheld tokens are permanently burned upon execution, reducing total circulating supply continuously.
Is the Mint or Freeze Authority active?
No. Mint Authority and Freeze Authority have been permanently revoked (set to None). No additional $pQT tokens can ever be created, and user accounts can never be frozen.
How does the 15% Crank Bounty incentive work?
Anyone can trigger the harvest_and_distribute contract instruction. When invoked, 15% of all accumulated fees across token accounts are instantly sent to the transaction caller's wallet as a bounty.
Where can I safely buy $pQT tokens?
